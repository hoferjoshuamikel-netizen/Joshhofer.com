02 / Featured project

Plant Wizard.

An automated growing system, designed for a smaller space.

Early design study01 / 02

01 / The idea

A compact
vertical farm.

Plant Wizard began as a proposal to automate everyday plant care: watering, lighting, environmental monitoring, and plant interaction in a compact enclosure.

The early design explored a cylindrical growing arrangement, with room for a motion system and individual plant monitoring.

Concept from our original project proposal.

02 / The system

More than
a grow light.

A second layout explored stacked growing trays. Both studies asked the same question: how can the structure, plant care, and automation work together?
