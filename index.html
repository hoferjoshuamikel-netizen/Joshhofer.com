02 / Featured project

Plant Wizard.

Hydroponics, controls, and robotics in one cabinet.

Final CAD model01 / 03

01 / The design

One cabinet.
Many systems.

Plant Wizard brings hydroponic growing, nutrient delivery, environmental sensing, lighting, and a moving camera into a refrigerator-sized appliance.

The final design combines three main growing modules and nursery positions with an aluminum frame, separate fluid reservoirs, and an X/Z camera gantry.

Final CAD / Wentworth capstone, 2026

02 / My contribution

pieces work.

I led electrical and systems integration: the control architecture, power distribution, sensor interfaces, and communication between the embedded controllers and the Jetson computer.

That meant turning component choices into schematics, wiring, controller responsibilities, and interfaces our mechanical and irrigation systems could work with.
